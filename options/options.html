Containers

Each container keeps its own saved cookie jar. The active container's cookies live in the browser; the others are stored by the extension.

Site assignments

Pages on an assigned domain open in that container automatically. More specific domains win.

Behavior

Grouping is off by default. Chrome and Brave cannot color individual tabs — tab-strip colors are only available inside tab groups. With grouping off, the toolbar icon stays the brand logo and tab markers identify containers.

Tab indicators

Mark container tabs without groups. Favicon and title markers are applied inside web pages; Chrome and Brave internal pages are not modified.

Favicon markers keep the site icon when the browser allows it; the dot adds a colored dot, while the title marker adds a colored initial badge and prefixes the full container name. If the icon cannot be read, a colored tile is used instead. Title markers change the page title. Burp headers only appear when traffic goes through a proxy; custom headers can affect cross-origin requests.

Multi-account workspace

About BURPEE-SWITCH

BURPEE-SWITCH Multi-account and container management for Chromium-based browsers extension that lets users manage multiple isolated browsing containers and switch between accounts in the same browser window, instead of opening separate browser profiles.

  • Open or move tabs into a container from the toolbar popup or context menu.
  • Assign domains, group tabs by container, and choose the tab indicator that fits your flow.
  • Send container labels to Burp Suite when you need to trace proxied traffic.
